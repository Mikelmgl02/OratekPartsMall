Invent Item Composition

Master → references → supplier items: every supplier item hangs from the Reference of its own code and brand, and one reference can have many items. The SKU table merges into OEM lines; OEM numbers that a catalog prints under one of its own items merge into one line (3b–3d); aftermarket codes become References once they reach one line, and the rest wait in the catalog ledger; library numbers and new supplier products are unpublished lines.
